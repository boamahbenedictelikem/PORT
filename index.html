About Me

I am a Product Designer and Front-End Developer who believes the best digital products start by listening deeply to real user frustrations.

Currently pursuing a BSc in Information Technology at the University of Ghana. Over the past 2+ years working in IT support and customer service at ERASEL Digital Hub, I’ve diagnosed computer problems and observed firsthand how ordinary users struggle with confusing interfaces.

That experience sparked my passion for UX design: combining visual design, front-end code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React), and user research to build software that is effortless and accessible for everyone.
